Common questions

How do I make a widget?
Open SmallWorks, tap Make, and describe the widget in plain words, for example "days until my trip to Lisbon". Check the result and tap to keep it.

How do I put it on my Home Screen or Lock Screen?
Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap the + button, choose SmallWorks, and add the widget. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add a SmallWorks widget.

The widget shows the wrong one of mine.
Touch and hold the widget on your Home Screen, tap Edit Widget, and choose the one you want.

Which iPhones does it work on?
iOS 26 or later. It works best with Apple's on-device intelligence. If your iPhone doesn't have it, SmallWorks offers to download an open model (about 1 GB or more), so keep some free space and use Wi-Fi.

Does SmallWorks see my calendar, health or location data?
Only your iPhone does. The app reads it on the device after iOS asks you, and never sends it anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

Why does a widget ask to "Allow" a website?
Live-data widgets (such as F1 or crypto prices) use one ready-made source. You see the real value first, and the widget only updates once you tap Allow.

How do I delete a widget?
Open the widget in SmallWorks and choose Delete. Also remove it from your Home Screen by touching and holding it and tapping Remove Widget.

How do I get rid of all my data?
Delete the app. Everything is stored on your iPhone and is removed with it.
