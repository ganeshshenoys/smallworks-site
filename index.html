    How it works

    A widget is a small spec, not code.

    The model on your iPhone doesn't write a program. It fills in a short form: what kind of widget, its words, its look. The app checks the form, and one native renderer draws it. So a widget can only do what the form allows.

    The model is Apple's own on-device one. If your iPhone doesn't have it, SmallWorks can download one open model and run it on the device instead.

    Privacy

    Your iPhone's data stays on your iPhone.

    • Calendar, Reminders and Health are read on the device, and only after iOS asks you.
    • No accounts, no analytics, no ads. SmallWorks has no server of its own.
    • A widget that needs the web gets one address, and only once you tap Allow.
    • Sharing a widget sends its design, never your counts or pictures.

    The details are in the privacy policy, written in plain words.
